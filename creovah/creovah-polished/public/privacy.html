Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This policy explains what information creovah ("we", "us") collects when you use our platform, how we use it, and the choices you have — including how to request deletion of your data. It applies to the creovah web app and any social media accounts you choose to connect to it.

Information we collect

We collect the information you give us directly, plus information from any social accounts you choose to connect.

CategoryExamples
Account informationName, username, email address, phone number, password (stored as a secure hash, never in plain text)
Profile & onboarding detailsYour role, how you found creovah, notification preferences
Connected social accountsInstagram/Facebook Page name, username, and an access token (encrypted at rest) that lets creovah publish or manage content on your behalf, only for the permissions you approve
Usage dataBasic technical information such as log data and device/browser type, used for security and troubleshooting

How we use your information

  • To create and secure your creovah account
  • To publish, schedule, or manage content on connected social accounts, at your direction and only within the scope you authorize
  • To operate core product features such as your dashboard and content calendar
  • To communicate with you about your account or changes to our service
  • To maintain the security and integrity of the platform

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use your connected social media data for advertising purposes.

How we share information

We do not share your personal information with third parties except:

  • With the social media platforms you explicitly connect (e.g. Meta/Instagram/Facebook), strictly to perform the actions you request
  • With service providers who help us run our infrastructure (for example, our database and hosting providers), under obligations to protect your data
  • If required by law, or to protect the rights, safety, or property of creovah or our users

Data from Meta platforms (Instagram & Facebook)

When you connect an Instagram or Facebook account, we request only the permissions needed to display your connection status and to publish or manage content at your instruction. Access tokens are encrypted before storage. We do not access, store, or share your Instagram/Facebook data for any purpose beyond providing the features you've enabled, and we comply with Meta's Platform Terms and Developer Policies.

Data retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active. If you disconnect a social account, we stop using and immediately delete the associated access tokens. If you request full account deletion, we delete your personal data as described below.

Your rights and how to request deletion

In-app: Go to Settings and select Request data deletion. This immediately disconnects any linked social accounts and revokes their access tokens, and submits a request to permanently delete your account data.

You can also email us directly at creovah@gmail.com with a data deletion or access request, and we'll respond as soon as possible.

If you disconnect or remove creovah from your Facebook or Instagram settings directly, Meta will notify us automatically and we will process the deletion of the associated connection data.

Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including password hashing and encryption of stored access tokens. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your data using accepted industry standards.

Children's privacy

creovah is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Email us at creovah@gmail.com.
