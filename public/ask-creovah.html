HELP CENTER

Frequently asked questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Start with the question closest to what you are trying to do.

What is Creovah?
Creovah is a social media publishing workspace for creating, preparing, scheduling and publishing content from one place.
Which social platforms can I connect to Creovah?
Creovah is built around connected social accounts including YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn, X, Facebook and Instagram. Available publishing features can vary by platform and account permissions.
Can I schedule social media posts with Creovah?
Yes. Creovah lets you prepare content, choose when it should go out, and keep upcoming content organized in your workspace.
Can I use hashtags and mentions?
Yes. Creovah supports hashtags and platform-specific tag or mention workflows where the connected platform and its API support them.
Can I customize a post for each social platform?
Yes. Creovah can provide platform-specific settings and content controls instead of forcing every network to receive exactly the same configuration.
What should I do if a social account will not connect?
Check that you are signing into the intended account, approve the requested permissions, and make sure the platform connection is available for your account. If the problem continues, reconnect the account and check the message shown by Creovah.
Can I disconnect a social account?
Yes. Connected social accounts can be disconnected from your Creovah settings.
Is Creovah free?
Creovah's available plans and pricing can change. Check the Creovah product pages for the current offering.
